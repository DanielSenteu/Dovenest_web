DoveNest x AMREF Flying Doctors

Africa's leading air ambulance, at your service.

Through our partnership with AMREF Flying Doctors, DoveNest Insurance Brokers offers you access to Africa's premier emergency medical evacuation service. Operating since 1957, AMREF Flying Doctors provides rapid air and ground ambulance transport to get you from the point of emergency to the nearest appropriate medical facility — fast.

With the Maisha Plan, you and your family are covered for unlimited evacuation flights and ground ambulance transfers throughout the year. Whether you are at home in Kenya or travelling across East Africa, AMREF Flying Doctors ensures that expert medical help is never far away.

Important: AMREF Flying Doctors covers emergency medical evacuation and transport only. Hospital bills and medical treatment expenses are not included. We strongly recommend pairing your Maisha Plan with a comprehensive health insurance policy through DoveNest for complete protection.
